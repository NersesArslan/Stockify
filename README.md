It's also a good time for me to step back and reflect on what counts as a quality business. Because truth be told, my instincts about investing in semiconductors has less to do with looking at the numbers showing me that they are exceptionally well managed businesses. There must be thousands of exceptionally managed businesses all aroudn the world at different sizes, across different industries. but none of them are providing investors with the kind of returns I'm seeing in certain parts of the software, tech and semi-conductor industries. 

My investment thesis is based on the observation of the events unfolding during the early stages of the AI boom. We are in the middle of a historical revolution that will upend the modern workplace, labor and the information economy. There is  surging demand not just for the Large Language Models that have been released since 2022, but the infrastructure (both software, cloud, platform and hardware, semi-conductor ecosystem, energy and data centers).

There is a once in a lifetime opportunity for investors alive at this period in history to buy shares in companies whose valuations will soar to higher levels because of scale at which this new technology will be integrated in every conceivable facet of modern life. But at the same time, there is going to be a lot of companies that will go under. There will be a lot of people starting companies with to build something while all this happening, but not all of them will survive. There are bottlenecks, fierce competition, and larger companies buying out smaller ones and major valuation concerns for companies that are already considered exceptionallt well managed.

My project is aimed at building a system to help filter our which companies are worth investing in, high quality companies with a reasonable valuation, from the ones that are either too risky or over valued because their market value prices in too much of their future success. I'm thinking of companies like Palantir, Tesla and more recently SpaceX. These may be exceptionally well run businesses that are part of the AI boom in some shape or form, but whose business model and sustainability may be called into question because of their dependence on narrative, charismatic CEOs, and government contracts. 

Quality comes first for identifying these business, valuation comes second.
